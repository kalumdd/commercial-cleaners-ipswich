Commercial cleaning by type of premises

Offices

Offices need clean desks, kitchens and washrooms every working day, with less frequent tasks such as glass, high-level dusting and carpet deep cleans spread through the month. Our office cleaners in Ipswich page has the full breakdown.

Retail units and showrooms

Shops need to look their best when the doors open. We clean early in the morning: floors, glass, fitting rooms, counters and customer toilets, with extra attention to entrances, where grit and rain come in.

Industrial units and warehouses

Industrial sites need different equipment and methods: scrubber driers for large floors, high-level dusting of racking and light fittings, and regular cleaning of canteens, offices and welfare blocks used by shift workers.

Shared and multi-tenant buildings

Managing agents and landlords need communal areas kept clean for every tenant: entrances, lifts, stairwells, shared kitchens and washrooms. We clean communal areas on their own or alongside individual tenants' units.

Schools, nurseries and medical premises

These sites need cleaning that follows infection control guidance, with colour-coded cloths and mops so equipment used in toilets never touches kitchen or clinical areas. Our contract cleaners in Ipswich page explains how we manage these sites.

What affects the cost of commercial cleaning

Commercial cleaning is priced on the hours needed to clean your premises to the agreed schedule. These factors decide those hours.

Size and layout
More floor space, rooms and floors take longer to clean.
Frequency
Daily cleaning costs more each month than two or three visits a week.
Washrooms and kitchens
These take the most time per square metre and drive the hours.
Type of premises
Industrial and clinical areas need specialist equipment and methods.
Consumables
Soap, paper and bin liners can be included or left to you.

Frequently asked questions

How much do commercial cleaners cost in Ipswich?

Pricing is based on the hours needed to clean your premises to the agreed schedule, so it depends on size, layout and how often you want cleaning. After a site survey we give you a fixed monthly price.

Do you supply cleaning products and equipment?

Yes. We bring all equipment and products, with COSHH data sheets for anything used on site. Washroom consumables can be supplied and restocked as part of the contract.

Can you clean outside business hours?

Yes. Most of our cleaning is early morning or evening. We can also provide daytime cleaners for busy sites that need washrooms and kitchens kept up through the day.

What happens if our usual cleaner is off?

We cover holidays and sickness with cleaners who already know your site, so the schedule carries on without you having to chase it.

How are your cleaners checked?

Before starting on any site, cleaners have their right to work and references checked. DBS checks can be arranged where a site needs them, such as schools and healthcare premises.

Can you clean during the day while we are working?

Yes. Some sites need a daytime cleaner to keep washrooms, kitchens and reception tidy. We can combine a daytime presence with a main clean before or after hours.

Do you clean windows?

We clean internal glass, partitions and the inside of windows as part of regular cleaning. External window cleaning, especially above ground floor, is usually arranged separately with a specialist.

How quickly can you start?

For most sites we can start within a couple of weeks of agreeing the schedule, once keys, access and any site inductions are sorted. One-off and deep cleans can often be booked sooner.
