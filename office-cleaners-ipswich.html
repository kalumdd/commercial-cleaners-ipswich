What our office cleaning covers

A typical office clean covers the areas your team uses every day: desks and touchpoints, kitchens, washrooms, meeting rooms and floors. We agree exactly what is included and how often, then put it in writing.

We clean for accountants, solicitors, estate agents, tech firms and serviced office providers across Ipswich, from single-room offices on Museum Street to multi-floor buildings on the Waterfront. Part of our wider commercial cleaning in Ipswich.

A clean office affects more than appearance. Shared desks, kitchens and washrooms are where colds and stomach bugs spread around a team, so regular cleaning of touchpoints such as door handles, kettles, fridge doors and phones helps keep people healthy and at work.

We fit our visits around your working hours. Most offices are cleaned early in the morning or after everyone has gone home, so cleaning never interrupts calls, meetings or visitors.

Office cleaning in Ipswich: what to expect

Daily tasks

The core of every office clean: emptying bins, cleaning kitchens and washrooms, wiping clear desks and touchpoints, and vacuuming or mopping floors. These are the tasks people notice when they are missed, so they are done every visit.

Weekly and monthly tasks

Some jobs do not need doing every day but make a big difference when done regularly: high-level dusting, cleaning internal glass and partitions, wiping skirting boards and descaling kettles. We spread these through the week so each visit stays efficient.

Periodic deep cleans

Carpets in busy offices benefit from a deep clean every few months, and kitchens and washrooms from a deeper clean of grout, extractor fans and behind appliances. We schedule these in advance and can do them at a weekend so the office is not disrupted.

Clear desk policies

We wipe desks that are cleared at the end of the day. If papers and personal items are left out, we clean around them instead of moving anything. A clear desk policy helps your cleaners do a better job and keeps documents secure.

Security and keys

Most office cleaning happens out of hours, so key and alarm handling matters. Keys and fobs are logged, alarm codes are limited to the cleaners on your site, and you can change codes or ask for a key audit at any time.

Serviced and shared offices

For serviced office providers and shared buildings, we clean individual suites to different schedules, along with communal areas such as receptions, kitchens and washrooms.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to be there when the cleaners come?

No. Most clients give us keys or a fob and an alarm code. Keys are logged and stored securely, and you can ask for a key audit at any time.

Can we change what is cleaned?

Yes. The schedule is reviewed with you and can be changed with a week's notice, for example adding extra visits during busy periods.

Do you clean IT equipment?

We dust monitors and wipe keyboards and phones with suitable products. We do not open or move IT hardware.

Is there a minimum contract length?

We agree an initial period with you at quote stage. After that, the contract runs month to month.

How long does an office clean take?

A small office of up to 10 desks often takes one to two hours per visit. Larger offices take longer, and we may send more than one cleaner so the work fits your cleaning window.

Can you supply toilet roll, soap and bin liners?

Yes. We can supply and restock washroom and kitchen consumables as part of your contract, or use stock you buy yourself.

Do you clean on bank holidays?

We agree bank holiday cover with you in advance. Most offices that close on bank holidays skip that visit, while others prefer a clean the evening before they reopen.

What if we are not happy with a clean?

Tell your named contact and we will put it right, usually at the next visit or sooner if it is urgent. Every issue is logged and checked off so it does not happen again.
